Our Story

Founded in 2010, Natural View began as a passion project by a group of nature enthusiasts and professional photographers. What started as a small blog sharing photos from weekend hikes has grown into a comprehensive platform showcasing the world's most breathtaking natural landscapes.

Our mission is to connect people with nature through stunning photography, inspire conservation efforts, and promote sustainable tourism. We believe that by showcasing the beauty of our planet, we can encourage more people to appreciate and protect these natural wonders.

Today, our team consists of over 20 professional photographers and writers who travel the world to capture and document these incredible locations. We partner with conservation organizations and local communities to ensure our work has a positive impact.
